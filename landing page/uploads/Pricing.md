# **04  Strategic Context**

## **Current Business Model**

Ape Gym currently operates on a WOM approach, and subcontracts Personal Trainer taking 30% commission for the Client addressed.

| Revenue Stream | Pack 3x week | Pack 4x week | Notes |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Client** | $150 week / $600 month | $175 week / $700 month | The single session is $50. The pack 4x week, client’s receive 12.5% discount |
| **Client via hired trainer** | $45 week / $180 month | $52.50 week / $210 month | Alexandre keeps 30%; trainer receives 70% |

## **Suggested Business Model**

As a small business targeting to digitalise and scale, using the product as the core infrastructure of the business, the hybrid model with a product-led approach would work well in a B2C level, while sales-led approach would be most suitable for enterprise level (B2B).

### **B2B Monthly Pricing**

The WOW Gym is a chain of gyms in the USA with 9 units. Ape Gym charges 30% commission per Client member of WOW’s Gym to run its business inside the Gym, in order of a potential improvement in acquisition, brand awareness and positioning against competitors, apart from increasing retention and customer loyalty.
